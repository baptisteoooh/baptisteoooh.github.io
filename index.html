Stage Scraper

A personal internship search assistant, built by an engineering student.

What it is

Stage Scraper is a personal, non-commercial project I am building during my 4th year of engineering studies (ESILV, Paris, MedTech & Health major). I am looking for an internship in medtech and digital health, ideally in AI and data roles, and offers are scattered across dozens of websites.

What it does

  • Searches internship offers in the Île-de-France region from a few job search sources.
  • Removes duplicates and ranks offers against my own profile.
  • Helps me keep track of my applications.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public job board: offers are not republished or displayed to anyone else.
  • It runs locally on my own computer, with a single user (me) and a low volume of searches.
  • It has no commercial purpose and no advertising.
Student project, 2026–2027.
